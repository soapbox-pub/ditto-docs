# Getting Started with Ditto

Getting started takes about 2 minutes. Here's everything you need to know.

## Step 1: Get a Nostr signer

Before using Ditto, you need a way to manage your Nostr keys. The recommended approach is to use a **browser extension signer** -- it holds your private key securely and signs events on your behalf.

Popular options:

- **[Alby](https://getalby.com)** -- Browser extension with built-in Lightning wallet
- **[nos2x](https://github.com/fiatjaf/nos2x)** -- Lightweight Chrome extension
- **[Nostore](https://apps.apple.com/app/nostore/id1666553677)** -- Safari extension for macOS/iOS

Install one, create a new key (or import an existing one), and you're ready.

## Step 2: Open Ditto

Head to **[ditto.pub](https://ditto.pub)** and click **Join** or **Login**.

Your signer extension will ask you to approve the connection. Once you do, you're in.

## Step 3: Set up your profile

Click your avatar in the sidebar to edit your profile:

- **Display name** -- What people see
- **Bio** -- Tell the world about yourself
- **Avatar & banner** -- Make it pretty
- **Lightning address** -- So people can zap you

## Step 4: Find your people

Nostr is a global network. Here are some ways to discover people:

- Browse the **Global** feed to see what everyone's posting
- Check the **Community** feed for curated content
- Use **Search** to find people by name or NIP-05 address
- Look for **Follow Packs** -- curated lists of accounts to follow

## Step 5: Make it yours

Head to **Settings > Appearance** to customize your experience:

- Pick a **theme** (or build a custom one)
- Toggle which **content types** show in your feed
- Configure your **relays** for maximum reach
- Set up your **Lightning wallet** for zapping

## You're in!

That's it. Start posting, start following, start exploring. Welcome to the network.
