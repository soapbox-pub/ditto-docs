# What is Nostr?

Nostr stands for **Notes and Other Stuff Transmitted by Relays**. It's an open protocol for decentralized social networking -- and it's simpler than you might think.

## The elevator pitch

Imagine social media where **you** own your account. Not Twitter. Not Meta. Not anyone. Your identity is a cryptographic keypair that lives on your device. You can take it anywhere, use it with any app, and no one can ban you from the network.

## How it works

### Keys, not accounts

Instead of a username and password, you get two things:

- **A public key** (your identity -- like an email address anyone can see)
- **A private key** (your secret -- like a password that proves you're you)

That's it. No email verification. No phone number. No selfie with your ID. Just math.

### Relays, not servers

Your posts don't live on one company's servers. They get sent to **relays** -- simple servers that store and forward messages. You can use as many relays as you want. If one goes down or kicks you off, your data lives on all the others.

Think of relays like email servers. You can send mail from Gmail to Outlook because they speak the same protocol. Nostr works the same way -- different apps, different relays, same network.

### Apps, not platforms

Because Nostr is a protocol (not a platform), there are many apps that speak it. Ditto is one of them. You can use Ditto today, switch to another Nostr app tomorrow, and all your followers, posts, and messages come with you.

**Your identity is portable. Your data is portable. Your social graph is portable.**

## Why does this matter?

| Traditional Social Media | Nostr |
|---|---|
| Company owns your account | You own your keys |
| Posts live on their servers | Posts live across many relays |
| They choose the algorithm | You choose the experience |
| They can ban you | No central authority |
| Locked into one app | Works with any Nostr app |
| Your data is their product | Your data is yours |
